# Quantitative Strategy Engine v4.5.0 implementation plan

**Status:** Seven-phase implementation completed for v4.5.0 on September 30, 2026. This file remains the design baseline; delivered behavior and verification are documented in [the v4.5.0 engine guide](quant_strategy_v4.5.0.md).

## Purpose and delivery boundary

v4.5.0 will turn the Quantitative Strategy Engine into a measurable, adaptive **paper trading** system. It will use available historical data to generate and explain decisions for the four enabled categories (equities and ETFs, options, crypto spot, and Event contracts), execute only qualifying simulated trades, grade closed trades after costs, and compare observed portfolio results with the saved 18.5% annual return objective. The objective is a scorecard, not a mandate to manufacture trades or a profitability claim. Futures remain disabled unless the administrator separately enables them.

The release will make one integration change to the main app: it will call a separately managed, versioned strategy service for signal decisions. Thereafter the daily AI process can inspect and change that service's strategy source and activate a validated revision without rebuilding, committing, releasing, or restarting the main app. The main app retains market-data validation, user authorization, capital accounting, paper fills, settlement, and non-overridable risk controls. Neither AI nor the strategy service can submit a real order.

**Cost boundary:** No new market-data subscription or historical-data purchase is part of v4.5.0. Reuse existing Webull and public-data access; record any entitlement denial rather than buying access. Existing AI providers may charge for inference, so show usage and enforce a saved daily request/cost ceiling. Local storage and compute also have capacity limits; “$0 path” means no new data purchase, not unlimited infrastructure.

## 1. Collect the data the strategies actually need

### Equities and ETFs

1. Use the existing Webull stock-bar adapter in `services/portfolio_strategy_data.py` for the ten saved symbols and SPY benchmark. Request up to the provider's supported 1,200 completed daily bars where the current entitlement permits; preserve session dates, split-adjustment convention, source, and retrieval time. The existing 260 bars already satisfy the current 200-day indicator warm-up, so extra bars improve testing rather than unblock live evaluation.
2. Cache completed bars by session. A new daily signal is computed only from data available by that session's close; execution at the next open must use a fresh quote and the then-current risk/account state. Intraday strategies, if added later, must use actual completed intraday bars rather than repeatedly treating yesterday's daily indicators as new evidence.
3. Persist one decision per symbol, strategy version, and evaluation time: input snapshot hash, source and availability timestamps, indicator values, each entry condition, proposed action, final risk disposition, and any later paper fill. Retain no-trade decisions as first-class records.

### Options: build our own forward history

1. Keep collection scoped to the five saved option underlyings: SPY, QQQ, IWM, NVDA, and TSLA unless the administrator changes the watchlist. Use the existing `research_captures` archive and option collector rather than a new paid feed. A watched underlying is not a fixed contract: catalog the changing expirations and strikes needed for 20–65 DTE spreads, prioritizing expirations near the configured 45 DTE and enough surrounding strikes to construct both legs.
2. For every requested contract, retain the exact contract ID, underlying and underlying quote, expiration, strike, put/call, multiplier, bid and ask with reported sizes, quote/provider and receipt times, IV and Greeks where actually supplied, volume and open interest where supplied, catalog page/completeness evidence, source/environment, request outcome, and a checksum of the credential-redacted raw response. Preserve missing fields as missing. Keep immutable raw captures alongside normalized, versioned observations.
3. Continue the existing near-close ATM call/put measurement under its own methodology and source ID. Repair `services/research_jobs.py` so the daily IV job reads only option catalog/quote captures for the relevant close window and underlying, partitions work by symbol/day, and never sends unrelated Event/crypto records through the general 100,000-record dataset builder. Keep the general dataset cap for broad research jobs. Retry failed dates only when their original captures meet the same completeness and timing checks; make retries idempotent and show exclusions.
4. Verify each market day against the actual NYSE close, including early closes. A **valid options-history session** for an underlying requires complete relevant catalog evidence, a same-strike call/put pair near the target DTE, usable provider or explicitly labeled receipt timing within the close window, finite IV, and two-sided executable quotes for candidate spread legs. Record coverage separately for IV measurement and spread replay. A partial day never silently counts as a valid day.
5. Provide per-underlying coverage counters, gaps, estimated storage growth, checksum failures, endpoint denials, and next eligible date. The current 10 GiB archive cap and approximately 3.7 GiB already stored must be capacity-planned before enabling denser capture. Compress and deduplicate; export verified older raw batches to local cold storage if necessary, with retrieval and checksum verification. Do not delete existing history as part of this release.
6. Probe the already authorized Webull option historical-bars endpoint for the five underlyings with bounded requests. Store accessible price/volume bars as a separate historical source for research. Bars do not become historical bid/ask, IV, Greeks, or a verified `ATM_PAIR_V1` day. Record entitlement failure and stop that probe rather than adding a subscription.

**Exact activation threshold:** The no-purchase options fallback below can make its first paper decision after one verified in-session option chain/underlying snapshot and enough already available underlying bars for its indicators; no future options-history day is required. A separate **30-session IV-history rule** becomes eligible *per underlying* only after **30 valid completed NYSE sessions** under one compatible measurement version. Thirty sessions, roughly six market weeks without gaps, support a short-window IV percentile; they do not establish a one-year IV rank or profitability. Existing seven days count only if they pass the new validity check. The present **252-session annual IV-rank rule** stays labeled unavailable until 252 distinct compatible sessions exist. Missing sessions extend these dates rather than being filled with estimates.

The no-purchase limitation is explicit: Webull documents historical option OHLCV bars and current snapshots with IV/Greeks, while point-in-time historical chains with IV/Greeks are offered as premium products by [Alpha Vantage](https://www.alphavantage.co/documentation/) and [Cboe](https://datashop.cboe.com/option-eod-summary). No premium source is activated in v4.5.0.

## 2. Replace inactive entry logic with defined, testable paper strategies

### Equities and ETFs

Split the current `equity_signal()` conjunction in `services/portfolio_strategy_signals.py` into two independent, versioned entry setups:

- **Trend rotation:** Once per completed NYSE session, rank the ten watchlist symbols using completed 63-session momentum and strength versus SPY; require a positive trend and price above the 200-day average. The two highest qualifying symbols may propose entries or rotation at the next regular-session open. Existing module budget, one-position-per-symbol, size, stop, and portfolio circuit rules still decide whether an entry can fill. A symbol losing trend/rank or hitting its recorded stop proposes an exit.
- **Pullback:** Keep the configured two-day RSI and Bollinger-band setup as a separate entry path within a positive long-term trend. It no longer also has to win the rotation ranking. Evaluate it from completed daily bars, and revalidate the next-session quote before a paper fill. Its ATR stop and exit conditions remain explicit.

Use a single deterministic conflict rule if both setups select the same symbol, record which setup won, and prohibit duplicate exposure. Freeze initial thresholds only after chronological development/holdout tests. Do not lower a threshold merely to reach an activity quota. A day with no qualifying symbol still produces recorded decisions and reasons, not a misleading silent `READY` badge.

### Options during history warm-up

Add a separately named **underlying-history plus current-chain** paper strategy. Use completed underlying returns and realized volatility (for example, 20- and 60-session windows), trend, and current quoted ATM IV to identify a candidate volatility regime. Choose the direction of a defined-risk spread from the saved trend signal; use only fresh, same-expiration, two-sided quoted option legs and available provider Greeks for contract selection. Enforce spread-width, credit, depth, freshness, maximum loss, position size, allocation, and existing paper-only controls in the main app. Record the realized-volatility and current-IV measures separately; do not call their ratio “252-day IV rank.”

Backtest candidate entry/exit thresholds where point-in-time option observations actually exist, then begin **forward paper observation** when the current-chain readiness test passes. The short-history IV strategy can be evaluated and paper activated per symbol at 30 valid sessions; the 252-session strategy is a later research variant. Every version reports its own trade count, net result, and history basis. A thin or crossed market, absent Greeks, missing leg, or unavailable quote correctly produces no trade.

### Crypto and Event contracts

Move the current pure decision rules behind the same versioned strategy interface without changing their initial behavior. Crypto's completed-hour Donchian/dominance inputs and Event's fresh forecast/quote inputs remain distinct. Event forecast production, final quote revalidation, settlement proof, paper fees, and saved Event risk policy remain in the main app. The Event module must show `RISK_BLOCKED` with the current $35 drawdown limit and measured drawdown until the administrator deliberately changes that policy; AI cannot change it. Existing positions continue to be monitored and settled if strategy evaluation is unavailable.

## 3. Independently updateable strategy service

1. In the one-time v4.5.0 app release, add a client at `services/strategy_client.py` and adapters in `services/portfolio_engine.py` and `services/portfolio_event_execution.py`. Build a separate `crypto-quant-strategy.service` running under a dedicated restricted account. Store its source, immutable candidate versions, active-version pointer, and validation artifacts outside both app checkouts, for example under `/home/jcavallarojr/crypto_quant_strategies`. The service listens only on a permission-restricted local Unix socket; it has no public HTTP port, broker credentials, real-order endpoint, or write access to the app checkout.
2. Define a versioned request/response contract. The app sends bounded, credential-free market snapshots with `user_id` scoped by the app, module, watchlist symbol, `as_of`/`available_at`, source/timestamp basis, completed bars or exact option/Event observations, current paper position summary, saved strategy parameters, and snapshot hash. The service returns `ENTER`, `EXIT`, or `NO_TRADE`, instrument/legs/side, reason codes and indicator checks, code hash, and strategy version. The app rejects malformed, late, stale, unknown-version, or unauthorized proposals, recomputes all risk/size/fee/eligibility checks, and records both proposal and final disposition with an idempotency key. Separate restricted operations return the active source/diff, accept a candidate patch against a stated parent hash, return validation results, and atomically activate or roll back an approved version.
3. Extract existing deterministic signal code into an immutable baseline package and prove parity against saved fixtures before activating the service. Use a per-module active version so one bad strategy cannot replace all four. The app can keep serving and managing existing paper risk while the strategy service reloads or restarts. If the service is unavailable, **new entries fail closed** and the app still applies its fixed stops, risk circuit, existing-position management, and provider-confirmed Event settlement.
4. Keep AI-editable code limited to pure signal modules plus declared numeric parameters. Run candidate code in a restricted subprocess with a fixed dependency allowlist, no network, no broker/DB credentials, read-only market fixtures, CPU/memory/time limits, and no writable app paths. Reject imports or behavior outside that boundary. Runtime activation swaps an atomic, checksummed active-version pointer and reloads the service; it never edits Python files imported by Gunicorn or `runtime.py worker`.
5. Persist every candidate's parent hash, generated patch, AI model/provider, input-evidence hashes, prompt/version, tests, replay results, activation time, paper decisions, and rollback reason. Preserve the former active version for immediate atomic rollback. A strategy activation is an auditable service version change, not a main-app release or a Git commit.

## 4. Daily AI review, candidate generation, and activation

Run one durable review job in the main app worker at 00:30 Eastern **each calendar day**. Equity/options evidence uses the latest completed NYSE session; crypto/Event evidence uses the preceding declared 24-hour window, with only finalized outcomes counted. The app worker fetches the **exact active strategy source and diff history** through the local socket, combines them with bounded market-regime measures, all four modules' signal and rejection counts, paper fills/exits, net fees and P&L, drawdown, and data gaps, and calls the already configured AI provider through `services/ai_service.py`. It submits a proposed source patch to the strategy service for isolated validation. Provider keys stay in the app's existing encrypted credential path and never enter the strategy service or AI prompt. AI may adjust signal logic and bounded strategy parameters; it cannot alter main-app risk policy, saved Event loss limits, paper/live isolation, authentication, quote freshness, or reporting facts.

Daily **review is mandatory**; daily **code change is conditional**. A candidate is activated automatically only after all of these gates:

1. Source/AST allowlist, type/schema checks, deterministic unit and property tests, resource limits, and no real-order or secret access pass.
2. Replaying identical inputs yields identical decisions; timestamp tests detect look-ahead, stale quotes, cross-user data, and duplicate decisions. Baseline fixtures prove unrelated module behavior remains stable.
3. A chronological held-out replay with realistic spread, fees, delayed/failed fills, and shared capital does not breach the saved risk limits and improves a prespecified net-risk score versus the active version. Keep development, selection, and holdout windows separate; rerunning candidates against the same holdout does not turn it into fresh evidence.
4. At least 30 completed observation sessions and 20 closed paper trades in the affected module are required before **outcome-driven automatic promotion**. Before that, AI may produce candidates and run them in shadow, but sparse wins/losses cannot justify automatic performance tuning. The initial v4.5.0 deterministic strategies are introduced through the ordinary tested release, not via this self-promotion gate.
5. One accepted revision per module per day at most, with a cooldown and automatic rollback if runtime errors, missing decisions, risk violations, or data-quality failures exceed defined thresholds. A lack of qualifying candidate leaves the active version unchanged and records why.

Define the net-risk comparison and rollback thresholds in tests before observing candidate results. AI prose is an explanation and patch proposal; the deterministic gates decide activation. Record provider usage and stop further AI calls at the configured daily budget. Never request provider keys or full private account data in the AI prompt.

## 5. Outcome measurement and reports

1. Join each paper decision to any entry, exit, fee, realized P&L, and later finalized outcome using immutable IDs and the strategy version active **at decision time**. Keep open/unresolved positions out of win/loss counts; display unrealized marks separately. Show per-module and combined trade counts, win/loss/breakeven, net dollars, return on actual capital, maximum drawdown, cash exposure, and observed data gaps.
2. Compare the $50,000 starting paper bankroll against the saved 18.5% annual target (a $9,250 one-year gain before compounding) using the existing goal-tracking calculation. Report elapsed time, measured equity, target-path difference, and whether annualization/sample size is available. A short profitable streak is not evidence the annual objective is achievable. Compare to a passive, timestamp-matched benchmark and cash, with costs included.
3. Add an explicit **Master report every six elapsed hours** option alongside Off/Daily/Weekly; configure the personal instance to six hours during deployment because that is the stated requirement. Keep the Event operational six-hour setting separate and labeled. Show next due time, latest start/completion, failure reason, and whether AI interpretation succeeded. Continue publishing deterministic evidence if AI fails; surface that failure rather than calling the strategy healthy from scan counts alone.
4. Each Master report explains, in plain words, which category evaluated which symbols, how many setups qualified, what actually filled or closed, why others were blocked, whether strategy service or data collection is impaired, and what the daily AI review proposed/activated/rejected. An Event risk lockout and an options-history warm-up must be visible in the top summary. Reports cite record IDs and active code hashes.

## Acceptance criteria

- All four enabled categories produce timestamped, attributable decisions; a zero-trade period has recorded evidence and specific reasons.
- The equity strategy evaluates historical data once per completed daily signal and can place correctly sized paper entries on a later eligible quote. No trade-count quota is encoded.
- Option capture for each watched underlying shows exact valid/missing session counts, source and quality; the daily IV job completes within its bounded scope. The fallback can make qualified paper decisions using existing underlying history and fresh option data. The 30-valid-session and 252-valid-session modes cannot activate early or conflate their metrics.
- Daily AI reviews the results and active source, can produce a genuine code patch, and can activate a gated strategy-service revision with an audit trail and rollback **without rebuilding or releasing the main app**. If evidence is insufficient, it records a shadow candidate or no change.
- Main-app paper-only controls, risk limits, quote checks, and Event settlement remain authoritative under service failure or malicious/invalid candidate output. AI cannot modify those controls.
- The requested six-hour Master reports resume, distinguish completed AI analysis from deterministic facts, and show net performance against the 18.5% annual research objective without claiming unmeasured profitability.
- v4.5.0 is released and the personal instance passes the repository's release/deployment verification sequence.
